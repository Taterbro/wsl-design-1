# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS (user-selected; no build step).

## Users

Primary: institutional investors and capital allocators — family offices, development finance institutions (DFIs), financial institutions — evaluating Waterlight Group as a credible African-founded global investment platform.

Situation: first-contact due diligence; deciding quickly whether Waterlight is real, bankable, and worth a serious conversation.

Job to be done: assess credibility fast, verify claims, and reach a controlled path to an investment discussion or data-room access — without wasting time on a promotional shell.

Additional audiences the platform must also engage (confirmed in the founding brief, secondary to the primary): governments, corporations, technology partners, development institutions, project owners, talent, and media.

## Product Purpose

Waterlight Group is an African-founded global enterprise platform that builds, invests in, and scales businesses, infrastructure, and technologies addressing essential economic and human needs.

The website must function simultaneously as: corporate headquarters, investment gateway, partnership engine, project showcase, thought-leadership platform, and trust centre.

Success means: a globally credible digital identity that engages the listed audiences while retaining a distinctive African origin and global outlook — and converts qualified first contact into a serious investment conversation or data-room access.

## Positioning

An African-founded global enterprise platform whose mechanism is building, investing in, and scaling ventures, infrastructure, and technologies against essential economic and human needs — pairing distinctive African origin with global outlook. This combination (African founding + institutional-grade global platform) is the position a neighboring platform could not truthfully copy.

## Operating Context

- First-contact institutional due diligence: visitors arrive to verify, not to be entertained; credibility signals and verifiable structure outrank promotion.
- Evaluation flows toward controlled access: an investment discussion or data-room path, not open self-service.
- Six simultaneous site functions (headquarters, investment gateway, partnership engine, showcase, thought leadership, trust centre) must coexist without diluting the primary investor journey.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Structure-only at this stage: no finalized content exists yet; sections launch as clearly-marked placeholders pending verified material.
- Hard rule: nothing unverified, ever. No invented portfolio companies, AUM or financial figures, returns, partner names or logos, testimonials, press, or case studies. Every unverifiable claim must be visibly marked as placeholder until the user supplies verified facts.
- The user supplies verified copy, figures, names, and evidence per section as work proceeds.
- Open decision: mechanism and wording for data-room access / contact routing (not yet defined).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Waterlight Group.
- No existing logo, color, typography, or voice guidance has been established; voice and visual identity are open for later design work.

## Evidence on Hand

None today — structure only. Future work must not fabricate project names, figures, leadership bios, partner lists, press, or case studies; absence of evidence is a standing state to be represented honestly in the interface.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility before persuasion — every surface answers the due-diligence question first.
2. Nothing unverified ships as fact; placeholders are explicit, never disguised.
3. African origin and global outlook are stated together, never traded against each other.
4. The primary investor journey (assess → verify → request access) stays the spine; other audiences route through it without crowding it.
5. Institutional restraint: precision and evidence carry more weight than promotional volume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established yet; global/institutional audience implies broad device and locale coverage as a default assumption (not yet confirmed as a standard).
