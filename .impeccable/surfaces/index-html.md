# Homepage — Waterlight Group

**Scope:** one public homepage (web, plain static HTML/CSS/JS). **Mode:** Persuade.
**Audience:** institutional investors and capital allocators (family offices, DFIs, financial institutions) doing first-contact due diligence; secondary audiences route through the same spine.
**Job / action:** assess credibility fast, verify structure, reach a controlled investment discussion or data-room path. Primary actions route into Investment Opportunities and Partner With Us.
**Proof / content:** structure-only; nothing unverified ships. Placeholders are explicit, never disguised. No fabricated entities, figures, logos, or claims.
**Constraint:** brands Brookfield (investment discipline) and Hitachi (multi-sector clarity) are learn-from references only — never copied.

**Chosen direction:** The Foundation Stone — an institution incised into water-formed stone.
**Memorable moment:** the raking sun sweeps the carved wall as you scroll, revealing the V-cut inscription by shadow.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The homepage is the institution's cornerstone — a wall of water-formed stone where permanence is carved, not asserted. It refuses the stock-photo hero, logo row, and equal-card grid this category always ships.

**OWN-WORLD:** Spring-formed travertine ground (bone `#E6DCC6`, sediment bands `#C9B99B`) with carved-shadow relief `#2A2621`, patinated bronze `#7E5C2E`, and verdigris `#4E6B57`. Parts are cut stone: incised V-groove rules, bronze plaques as actions, inset carved panels. Display is an incised epigraphic capital (Forum); body is a sturdy grotesque (Archivo). A single carved datum line runs the page's full height; a raking light moves across the surface.

**STORY:** Within the first viewport a first-time institutional visitor understands Waterlight is African-founded, global, and built to last; they believe the group is real and structured; they route into Investment Opportunities or Partner With Us.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Full-bleed raking-lit travertine wall. `BUILDING A SUSTAINABLE FUTURE.` cut as a V-groove inscription read by shadow, subcopy set small in the groove, three bronze-plaque actions (Explore Waterlight Group · Investment Opportunities · Partner With Us) pinned along the datum line, navigation incised into a stone lintel above.
